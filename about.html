Who We Are

A Consultancy Built on Trust & Transparency

MBBS Education Orbit Fly is one of India's most trusted consultancies for MBBS admission in top Russian medical universities. Founded with a single purpose — to make quality medical education abroad accessible and affordable for every deserving Indian student — we have since grown into a nationwide network with 7 branch offices and over 300+ successfully placed students.

We are registered, recognized, and recommended by students and parents alike. Our Google rating of 4.0 ★ speaks not just to our admission success rate, but to the level of care, honesty, and personalized support we bring to every student's journey.

Unlike many consultancies that focus solely on paperwork, we stay with our students for all 6 years of their MBBS program — from the first inquiry call to the day they graduate and return to India as qualified doctors.

From The Director's Desk

A Message From Nek Mohammad

"Giving up is the only sure way to fail."

Dear Aspiring Medical Students,

Welcome to MBBS Education Orbit Fly — your gateway to world-class medical education in Russia. My name is Nek Mohammad, and I founded this consultancy with a single purpose: to ensure that no deserving student in India is denied their dream of becoming a doctor due to financial constraints or lack of guidance.

Over the years, I have personally witnessed thousands of bright young minds struggle to secure MBBS seats in India because of astronomical fees, hidden donations, and an unfair system. Russia offers an extraordinary alternative — NMC-approved universities, English-medium education, world-class infrastructure, and fees that are a fraction of what private medical colleges in India charge.

At MBBS Education Orbit Fly, we don't just process paperwork. We walk with you through every single step — from choosing the right university based on your profile and budget, to the day you graduate and return to India as a qualified doctor. Our team of experienced counsellors, legal experts, and on-ground coordinators in Russia ensures you are never alone on this journey.

I always tell my students: "Giving up is the only sure way to fail." No matter how difficult the road ahead seems — a visa hurdle, a documentation challenge, homesickness thousands of miles from family — our team is available to support you at every step.

With 300+ students successfully placed in top Russian medical universities, 7 branch offices across India, and a perfect 4-star Google rating, we have earned the trust of hundreds of families. That trust is our greatest honor — and our greatest responsibility.

If you are serious about your medical career and want transparent, honest guidance — reach out to us today. Your future begins with a single step, and we are honored to take that step with you.

Warm regards and best wishes,

Nek Mohammad
Founder & Director, MBBS Education Orbit Fly
