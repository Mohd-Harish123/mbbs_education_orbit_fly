What We Do

Everything You Need, Under One Roof

Free Counselling

One-on-one career counselling session to assess your eligibility, discuss NEET score, budget, preferred climate, and shortlist the best 2–3 universities for your profile. Available via call, WhatsApp, or in-office visit at any of our 9 branches.

Completely Free

Admission Assistance

Complete university application support — we fill and submit forms, liaise with the university's admission office, track application status, and secure your Offer Letter. We have direct contacts at our 9 partner universities to fast-track your application.

End-to-End

Document Attestation

We handle HRD Apostille attestation of all your academic documents (Class 10, 12, birth certificate), translation into Russian by certified translators, and notarisation. All documents are returned to you well before your visa appointment.

Govt. Certified

Invitation Letter Processing

We apply for the official Invitation Letter (Form 1B) from the Russian Ministry of Internal Affairs on your behalf — a critical document without which the student visa cannot be applied. We track the application and ensure timely delivery.

Ministry of Internal Affairs

Visa Application Support

We prepare your complete student visa dossier (D-type visa), guide you through the Russian Embassy appointment booking process, brief you on what to expect at the visa interview, and ensure 100% document completeness to avoid rejection. Our visa success rate is 100%.

100% Success Rate

Flight Booking

We research and book the most affordable direct or connecting flight to your university city at the best available fare. Group bookings with other students heading to the same city are arranged whenever possible to reduce costs and build a companion network before arrival.

Best Fare Guarantee

Airport Pickup

Our representative (often a senior Indian student or local coordinator) meets you at the arrival terminal with a name board and takes you directly to the university hostel. No taxi confusion, no language barrier — a familiar face on your first day in a new country.

Included

University Enrolment Support

Within the first 3 days of arrival, we help complete all university registration formalities, hostel check-in, student ID card, timetable collection, and introduction to the faculty and student body. We ensure you start classes confidently on Day 1.

On-Ground Support

SIM Card & Banking

We assist you in getting a local Russian SIM card (for internet and local calls) and help you open a student bank account to receive money from India through official banking channels. We also guide you on the best money transfer methods (low-fee options).

Post-Arrival

FMGE / NExT Coaching

Starting from Year 4, we provide structured FMGE (and upcoming NExT) preparation — a comprehensive question bank, subject-wise video lectures, weekly mock tests, and doubt sessions with doctors who cleared FMGE themselves after studying in Russia. Our students consistently outperform the national average pass rate.

Above-Avg. Pass Rate

Accommodation Guidance

We provide detailed guidance on university hostel facilities, room options, and costs before you travel. For students who prefer private accommodation in later years, we connect them with trusted Indian seniors who help find safe, affordable flats near the university.

Safe & Verified

Medical Insurance

We arrange comprehensive student medical insurance valid in Russia as required by the university and visa regulations. The policy covers hospitalisation, emergency treatment, and outpatient care for the academic year at very affordable premium rates.

Mandatory Cover

24/7 Student Helpline

Our dedicated student support team is available round-the-clock via WhatsApp, phone, and email. Whether it's an academic query, a hostel issue, a medical emergency, or homesickness — our counsellors and ground staff in Russia are always just one call away. We never leave a student alone.

Always Available
