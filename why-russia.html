Russia — The Smart Choice

The World's Best-Kept Secret in Medical Education

Russia has been training medical students for over 200 years. Today, it is home to some of the world's finest medical universities — many ranked in global top 500 — offering a rigorous 6-year MBBS programme entirely in English.

With tuition fees starting at just ₹3.5 Lakh per year and living costs under ₹1 Lakh per year, Russia offers an affordable path to becoming a doctor — without compromising quality. Thousands of Indian doctors practising today completed their education in Russia.

Eligibility Criteria

Who Can Apply?

Must Have

  • Passed Class 12 (10+2) with Physics, Chemistry & Biology
  • Minimum 50% aggregate marks in PCB (45% for SC/ST/OBC)
  • Valid NEET score (mandatory for NMC recognition since 2020)
  • Age 17–25 years as on December 31 of admission year
  • Valid Indian passport (or in process of obtaining)

Not Required

  • No NEET cut-off rank pressure (only qualifying score needed)
  • No separate entrance exam conducted by Russian universities
  • No donation or capitation fees whatsoever
  • No Russian language knowledge required at time of admission
  • No interview or management quota pressure

Important NMC Update: As per NMC regulations effective 2020, all Indian students must have a valid NEET score to be eligible for admission to foreign medical universities and to appear for FMGE/NExT screening exam upon return to India.

After MBBS

FMGE / NExT & Your Future Career

1

FMGE / NExT Screening Exam

After completing MBBS from Russia, graduates must clear the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) — or the upcoming NExT exam — to obtain an Indian medical licence. Our structured coaching programme (FMGE Prep) starts from Year 4.

2

Passing Rate & Preparation

Students from NMC-recognised universities have a consistently improving FMGE pass rate. With focused preparation — which we facilitate — our students achieve above-average results year after year.

3

Career After Clearing FMGE

Once registered with NMC, you can practise anywhere in India, pursue PG (MD/MS) via NEET-PG, work in government hospitals, or proceed to USA (USMLE), UK (PLAB), or Middle East.

4

Our FMGE Support

We provide dedicated FMGE question bank access, mock tests, video lectures, and mentoring from doctors who cleared FMGE themselves after studying in Russia.
